# Decision 6 says this adapter writes no delivery record, and that is no longer true

Decision 6 of this repository, "A task handler runs inside the engine's own job transaction", ends
with two sentences about the delivery log:

> The consequence is that the engine's retry IS the recovery here, which is why this adapter
> contributes nothing to the delivery log of the platform: a redelivery proves that nothing was
> committed.

and, a paragraph later:

> on the application's datasource nothing of that happens, because there is nothing a record could
> add.

Story 879 of the platform changed that. A delivery which names no id now gets a record of its own.
The record takes no part in the deduplication, and it still answers the two other questions a
record answers: which BPMS holds the task, and which kind of id its id is. So this adapter writes a
record in both datasource modes, and for a user-task notification it writes one in both modes too.
What has not changed is the deduplication: on the application's datasource nothing is deduplicated,
and neither is a user-task notification on an engine of its own.

The reasoning of the decision still holds. Only the sentence drawn from it is too wide: it says
"nothing is written down" where the truth is "nothing is deduplicated". Stephan decides whether
decision 6 is superseded by an entry which says it that way, or whether the two sentences stay as
they are because the wiki carries the detail.

Where this is referred to:

- `DECISIONS.md`, decision 6, the two sentences quoted above.
- `camunda7-adapter.wiki/Configuration.md`, section "Deliveries are deduplicated on an own
  datasource, and need not be on the default one". Story 881 corrected the two sentences there
  which said that no record is written on the default datasource.
- `camunda7-adapter.wiki/Transactional-behavior.md`, sections "What did NOT change" and "A
  user-task notification still runs again". Story 881 added that a record is written there even
  though nothing is deduplicated.
- `integration-tests/.../Camunda7RepeatedDeliveryIT.java`, the class javadoc and
  `repeatedDeliveryOnTheSharedDataSourceRunsTheHandlerAgain`. The test asserted that the shared
  datasource records nothing and now asserts the one record and what it carries.
- `integration-tests/.../Camunda7UserTaskDeliveryRecordIT.java`, the new test class of story 881.
